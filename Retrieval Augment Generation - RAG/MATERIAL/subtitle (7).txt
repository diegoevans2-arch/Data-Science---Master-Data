Retrieval Augmented Generation is hopefully already starting to feel more approachable. Its goal is to make LLMs more useful and more accurate by giving them access to information that they might not have had when being trained. In order to do this, the LLM is paired with a collection of data called the Knowledge Base. This data might be private, recent, or just highly specific, and so not included in an off-the-shelf LLM's training data. When users submit prompts, they're first routed to the Retriever. The Retriever searches the Knowledge Base for relevant documents and adds text from those documents to the user prompts. LLMs are able to incorporate this information from the prompts into the responses they generate. The retrieved information grounds the final response, making it more accurate and relevant.
