In this module, you'll learn foundational RAG concepts, get familiar with a RAG system's main components, and start building a functional RAG system. Here's what you can expect to see. First, you'll get a high-level introduction to RAG, including what it is, why you'd want to use it, and how it can improve the quality of responses generated by a large language model. Next, you'll get a closer look at the architecture of a RAG system. RAG pairs an LLM with a retriever, a component that can help the LLM find relevant information in a knowledge base of trusted information. You'll do a deep dive on each component and then see how they work together. RAG is useful in a wide variety of contexts. So then you'll see several examples of production RAG systems, and start reflecting on opportunities to use RAG in your own LLM-based projects. Throughout the module, you'll get a hands-on with sample code, and at the end, you'll complete your first programming assignment, implementing parts of a simple RAG system. Throughout the rest of the course, you'll expand on this first system, adding more complex features like a robust retriever, a vector database, more sophisticated uses of an LLM, and a variety of techniques for monitoring and evaluation.
